RecipAI

A recipe box for your phone. Scale any ingredient and the whole recipe follows, time each step, and pull recipes in from anywhere.

  • Swipe from the left edge of the screen to go back.
  • Swipe a row left in list view for Favorite and Delete.
  • Press and hold a card for quick actions.
  • Tap + to write a recipe, or the center button to import one.
  • Paste a recipe or link anywhere to import it.
  • Drag a sheet down to dismiss it.
