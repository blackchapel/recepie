Grandma’s Banana Bread

Posted on May 4 · 23 comments

This is the loaf I grew up with: moist, not too sweet, and perfect for using up spotty bananas.

Serves 10

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • ⅓ cup melted butter
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x5 inch loaf pan.
  2. Mix the butter into the mashed bananas, then stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla.
  3. Sprinkle over the baking soda and salt, then mix in the flour until just combined.
  4. Pour into the pan and bake for 60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool on a rack for 15 minutes before slicing.
